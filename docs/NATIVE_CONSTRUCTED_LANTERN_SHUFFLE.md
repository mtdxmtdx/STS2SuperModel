# Declared TheLanternKey initial-shuffle certificate

This is an acceleration of the existing declared event-owner tape prior. The
prior, event options, source policy, native setup, stopping rule, primitive tape
law, source-generation identity and conservative source family are unchanged.
It is not a natural-run posterior or an event-selection proposal.

The enabled implementation/profile versions are respectively
`nosl-constructed-native-event-tape-shuffle-v2-public-evidence-v2` and
`owned-constructed-native-event-tape-shuffle-v2-public-evidence-v2`.
`enableConditioning=false` retains the historical rejection implementation and
profile verbatim. The enabled implementation also uses ordinary rejection when
its startup certificate fails; eligibility does not remove source attempts or
change their declared content.

## Native order and scope

The only supported owner remains the frozen TheLanternKey/Hive declaration,
including its explicit fixture floor and KEEP_THE_KEY then FIGHT public rule.
`NativeConstructedEventOwnerSetup.ValidatePublicOwner` verifies the actual event
parent, complete combat child, exact visible options and both recorded choices.
The initial run-start gap remains present, natural combat count remains null,
and no map observation is created. Ordinary and natural owner checks are not
relaxed.

The pinned source establishes this order:

1. `EventRoom.EnterInternal` begins the real event and prepares its canonical
   MysteriousKnight combat. `CombatRoom.Prepare` attaches players and creates the
   enemy. Its inherited FlailKnight A10 HP range is the singleton 108. This
   happens before the bridge's `CombatEntering` callback
2. TheLanternKey's KEEP option changes the event page. FIGHT constructs a mutable
   LanternKey and an unresolved `SpecialCardReward`, requests the already
   prepared encounter and suspends the event. The reward constructor and its
   Populate method do not add the card to the deck; only OnTake does that
3. RunDriver attaches the real observer/choice source, calls CombatEntering and
   enters the prepared combat. `CombatEngine.StartCombatAsync` runs
   `Player.PopulateCombatState` and its ordinary shuffle, initializes the fixed
   initial RAM move, then runs room-entry and monster hooks before the first draw
4. MysteriousKnight's sealed AfterAddedToRoom calls its no-op inherited base and
   applies self Strength 6 followed by self Plating 6. In solo play Plating's
   AfterApplied leaves the amount unchanged. Its first player-side hook grants
   enemy block 6. These powers and RAM startup do not move cards or consume
   shuffle words

All existing card, relic and potion hook/metadata guards still apply. They close
the listeners invoked by native power/block commands. The additional power
exceptions are restricted to this named startup entry point: Strength's damage
modifier and Plating's reviewed side-turn block/amount hooks. No new power is
added to the general draw-prefix closure.

The certificate requires one initial player decision, local revision zero, no
player powers, and one slot-zero MysteriousKnight with HP/maxHP 108, block 6,
exact self Strength/Plating public state, and the native Attack 17 × 1 RAM intent
in both the first snapshot and startup history. The two power facts must retain
their native order, amount, target and source slots. Generic v1-v5 startup
certificates continue rejecting this owner/monster combination.

## Reused proposal and density

`CreateConstructedLanternStartup` rebuilds the initial certificate packet from
the original typed combat facts, without altering the recorded evidence or its
parent. It emits exactly one initial-shuffle input. It supplies no HP,
CorpseSlug, Toadpole, reshuffle or later-draw plan.

The existing `NativePublicCombatPrefixProposal` arms the shuffle only after the
native CombatEntering callback. Its runtime guards verify the exact entry JSON,
owned player/run/shuffle stream, native Draw-pile membership, DeckVersion and
public upgraded-card multiset. The HP count is zero: attaching the existing HP
proposal here would miss HP creation during event preparation and is neither
necessary nor permitted by this certificate.

Every compatible startup has the observed prefix of that same native initial
permutation. Consequently the existing `ConditionalShuffleProposal` samples
physical copies, including equal visible signatures, and uses its unchanged
53-bit native bucket sizes, full-word consumption, exact rational likelihood
ratio, root-fixed envelope and correction. The fixed event path and deterministic
Knight startup add no selection/density factor. All other native tape addresses
remain on their original law. Previsited/conflicting conditioned words remain
errors; they are not silently retried as ordinary mismatches.

Acceptance still requires successful complete native setup, exact equality of
the entire full-v5 packet and its event/combat evidence, complete proposal hooks,
and the existing density correction. Inference receives the detached packet and
frozen declaration after source disposal, never the actual source recipe or
native graph. Forks replay their own accepted recipe and native owner lifecycle.

## Explicit limits and checks

Only the initial draw is conditioned. Plating and MysteriousKnight later turns
remain outside the draw-prefix closure, and unknown cards/relics/powers remain
ineligible. Ineligibility keeps ordinary replay rejection, its all-attempt ledger,
absence, cancellation and engine-error distinctions. No throughput or ranking
claim follows from a small engineering fixture.

Focused verification covers a fixed mixed Silent deck with upgrades/duplicates,
unchanged owner prior/source/family bytes, native draw/power/HP state, full public
equality and independent forks, exact metadata/reference mode, malformed startup
and owner boundaries, unknown entry hooks and uncertified fallback. Existing
finite-bucket/physical-copy shuffle proofs are reused unchanged. The retained
owner lifecycle tests continue exercising the explicit historical rejection mode.
