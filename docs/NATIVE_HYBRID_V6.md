## New native boundaries

multiset jointly with the intervening observed draw prefix. A source-pinned
transition closure proves that only ordinary draws changed that pile. The
proposal samples distinct physical cards, including upgraded or enchanted
copies, through the original sorted native shuffle and exact raw-word buckets.
A label-only marker identifies CardPileCmd.Shuffle and acknowledges successful
native completion. Matching a card list and RNG alone is insufficient. See
[the witness, density and envelope](NATIVE_PUBLIC_RESHUFFLES.md).

The monster branch proposal captures the original weighted-branch evaluations;
it neither re-evaluates getters nor replaces native selection. Exact float
preimages retain rounding, zero-weight endpoints, discarded bits and failure
tails. Public shape/slot/turn targets determine the requested outcome. Fixed-root
envelopes retain latent repeat-state likelihood. Current certificates cover
SludgeSpinner's reviewed rolls and LeafSlimeS's certified startup; unsupported
turns and models retain ordinary replay. The same-root reshuffle certificate can
extend the proved intent-publication endpoint. See [the weighted branch proof](PUBLIC_MONSTER_BRANCH_INTENTS.md).

The public first normal weak-slime roster selects native tickets for all three
original factory calls, including the bound-one draw. Every compatible full
word remains possible. Its exact root-fixed mass cancels with its envelope;
factory/run/room/stream ownership is explicit. See [formation conditioning](NATIVE_PUBLIC_WEAK_SLIME_FORMATIONS.md).

The draw-preserving certificate is now `nosl.public-first-draw-cycle.v3`.
NeowsFury, Strangle/StranglePower, Finisher and SuckerPunch cross their reviewed

## Ownership and failure accounting

The owning tape creates every proposal from detached public conditions and
separate auxiliary namespaces. All scopes, owner notifications, completion
checks, corrections and conditions survive exact hypothetical replay copies.
No actual source world, seed, graph or private move identifier enters belief
construction or student features. New diagnostics are optional audit fields.
The prior identity and legacy nonhybrid serialization remain unchanged.

A raw RNG counter advances before its callback can fail. Counters therefore do
not prove successful forcing. The native reshuffle marker waits for normal
completion, and the tape retains its first conditioned-word failure. Public
prefix checks, absent-world classification, completion and ReplayCopy check
that failure before proceeding. Source retry filters cannot convert it into a
later public mismatch or component null outcome; a later cancellation also
retains the earlier engine failure. A small internal test opener exercises those
source-loop paths. Production callers retain the native independent-recipe
factory; the opener is not a serialized option or a source-data input.

## Validation and bounded comparison

All four fixed native integration recipes (11002, 11004, 11006 and 11007)
exercise the actual owning scopes and exact fork replay through settlement.
They reuse known recipes to isolate integration and are not posterior acceptance
or fresh breadth evidence. The final aggregate passes 1,689 tests. The fresh Core
suite passes 4,493 with 3 existing opt-in skips; all 2,235 tracked Core/Core.Tests
files reconstruct exactly from the pinned upstream archive and local patches.

The repeated eight-root diagnostic retains the same prior, public input channel,
evaluation seeds, proposal bounds and 360-second cap as v4/v5. Its predeclaration
counts every failed or unexecuted source in the original denominator. Results
remain engineering-only; fixed-root corrections do not prove unbiased sampling
of roots selected by a wall-clock completion limit. A fresh predeclared development
cohort is still required before production enlargement or new fitting.


The isolated v6 probe completed all eight sources in 292.56 seconds (292.71
seconds including process overhead). It accepted 10 worlds and settled 64 of
96 candidate-world copies; the remaining 32 are ComputeTruncated, with zero
engine errors. Sources 11001, 11002, 11003, 11005 and 11008 completed. The
predeclared multi-action subset reached 3/6 complete, but neither required
combat-index-2 source completed, so the overall gate failed. All public inputs
and the prior identity equal v1. The 422 attempts include 175 component budget
exhaustions, 230 public mismatches, 7 density-correction rejections and 10
acceptances. They consumed 17,307 complete prefix trials and 2,419,818 callback
words; peak worker memory was 234,749,952 bytes.

For 11006 and 11007, the recorded first mismatches remain first-combat draws
after slime turns. For 11004, 49 nonexhausted proposals failed the entry check
before combat 1. That guard runs before the next stable-decision full-prefix
comparison, so this result does not identify which intervening reward, map or
event observation first differed. Early outside-combat comparison and further
source-reviewed conditional proposals are the next diagnosis. No source roots
were omitted or replaced, and no production rows or optimizer steps were added.
The v4 run overlapped other tests; its timing is not a controlled CPU comparison.
