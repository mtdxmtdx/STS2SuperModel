## Stable 203-root gate and continued pilot generation

The final runtime/identity corpus (`pilot-5000-v4`) passed its first global audit:
203 unique usable roots from 102 constructed source battles, with 187 fully valued,
14 partially valued and two auxiliary-only roots. Eight further all-masked roots
remain diagnostic records. All 211 raw references and all 407 attempt journals
verify, with no duplicate, invalid or orphan records. All 4,708 candidate/world

root-world draws, not 4,708 independent roots or independent hidden worlds.

The usable roots include 105 turn-one, 63 turn-two and 35 turn-three decisions,

47 card IDs, 11 potions, eight relics and four simple enemies. The source recipes
remain declared constructed curricula. General native-distribution readiness has
not been established. Sixty timeout attempts, 132 unavailable requested phases and
four unsupported setups remain separate from completed outcomes.

Four-world empirical regression targets exist, but strong confidence-certified
preference pairs remain zero and the objective remains uncalibrated. This gate
supports continuing the bounded empirical pilot, not formal training readiness.
The same frozen configuration has resumed toward 5,000 globally unique usable
roots on seven one-CPU processes; raw records, version hashes and attempt ledgers
are retained. See `configs/m5_phase203_report.json`.

The gate also exposes computational selection: all 60 timeout attempts entered
teacher labeling, 57 were turn-two/turn-three and all were nonstarter recipes
(38 single-card, 12 potion, 10 relic). None of the 168 starter attempts timed out.
Thus the retained-label distribution is easier than the attempted curriculum.
The bounded fit must be interpreted conditionally on successful labeling, and
30k/100k expansion is gated on this bias and unresolved-inclusive policy evaluation.
