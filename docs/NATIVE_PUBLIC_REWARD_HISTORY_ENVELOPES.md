# Public reward history envelopes

This optional certificate tightens existing card/resource rejection envelopes. It changes neither the declared native tape prior nor the proposal's word distribution. The source seed, source odds, source RNG state, sampled ratios, and private event/encounter identifiers are not certificate inputs. Full public equality and complete proposal validation remain necessary for acceptance.

## Closed prefix

`NativePublicRewardHistoryCertificate` uses detached complete public evidence and the existing first-three weak-encounter certificate. The initial native Silent A10 Neow selection must be one of FishingRod, LostCoffer, LeadPaperweight, WingedBoots, PreciseScissors, NewLeaf, ScrollBoxes, ArcaneScroll, Pomander, LavaRock, or NeowsTorment. The original six reviewed sources respectively upgrade a deck card every third normal fight, offer a Source.Other card reward and directly populated potion, offer Source.Other colorless cards, change travel eligibility, remove a deck card, or transform one deck card. The three added sources offer fixed common/uncommon bundles, grant one uniform rare card, or upgrade one selected starter card. None advances card-rarity or potion-presence pity during initial acquisition.

NewLeaf's pinned `AfterObtained` calls `CardCmd.TransformToRandom` with `Owner.RunState.Rng.Niche`. The default `CardFactory.CreateRandomCardForTransform` selects a candidate using one `NextItem` index and clones it; it does not use either reward-odds process or the player's Rewards stream. The native initial transform choice under owner0 and the ordinary NewLeaf inventory entry are therefore neutral to this certificate. A transformed card still passes the same actual-model hook checks at every public inventory snapshot, and any pending extra rewards still stop tightening through their displayed groups. The later ScrollBoxes/ArcaneScroll/Pomander extension below has its own stricter acquisition-boundary checks.

Only complete map owners, certified direct weak combats and their standard reward owners, the reviewed initial subchoices, complete unchanged ordinary shop leaves, and WoodCarvings, SunkenStatue, or RoomFullOfCheese/GORGE event boundaries are admitted. WoodCarvings makes explicit card transformations/enchantments; SunkenStatue gives a fixed relic or gold; Gorge creates a Source.Other/Uniform common-card choice. These event paths do not advance either pity process. Event identities are inferred from the existing pinned public option signatures, not read from a source runtime.

Every public inventory snapshot in the prefix must contain only the ordinary reviewed relic types (the eleven Neow positives, RingOfTheSnake, and SwordOfStone). Reflection verifies that the actual deck, relic, and potion model types inherit the native no-op reward creation/modification, potion-forcing, pre-offer, post-option-modification, alternatives and reroll-enabling hooks, except the explicitly guarded LavaRock case below. `RunState.IterateHookListeners(null)` enumerates precisely these model categories. Melted, wax, stacked, missing-status, and unknown relics decline the certificate.

A primary offer must contain exactly three own-pool base identities with unique canonical catalog identities. Extra/alternative/reroll groups, replaced snapshots, unsupported owners, gaps, missing intervening standard rewards, and unsupported inventories stop tightening. The already certified prefix remains useful; every target in the unknown suffix retains the original universal catalog/gold/potion envelope. In particular TheHunt can earn pending extra card rewards without overriding a listed reward hook. Native pending rewards are populated after primary generation and projected in the initial snapshot as Extra groups; that observable group closes this path conservatively.

## Exact factors

The pinned `CardRarityOdds.Roll` reads the current native float offset, then resets it to `-0.05f` on Rare or applies `min(offset + RarityGrowth, 0.4f)` otherwise. The initial offset is `-0.05f`. Legacy combat selection does not map a rolled rarity to another rarity, so every displayed base identity identifies the rolled rarity. All three cards advance the offset, including unpicked cards. The certificate constructs fresh standalone `CardRarityOdds` objects from these public-derived offsets to obtain the exact native thresholds. No hypothetical or source player's odds are read.

For slot i, let B_i be the exact high-word count whose native float conversion lies in the required rarity arm, and I_i the exact native index bucket count for the target identity in the remaining own pool. With Q=2^53, the local normalizer is z_i=B_i I_i/Q^2. The existing proposal already samples those rarity/index preimages and reports exactly z_i. The new slot envelope equals z_i, and the root envelope is their product. Upgrade words and every unrelated native draw remain unchanged.

`PotionRewardOdds.Roll` starts at `0.4f`, subtracts `0.1f` on a successful unforced roll and adds `0.1f` on failure, using native float arithmetic. The admitted inventories cannot force a potion. Every rarity in the fixed character/shared potion pool must be nonempty. Therefore absence identifies a failed presence roll, and a displayed potion identifies success. If p is the exact native float-less-than probability and m is the target potion identity mass, the displayed mass is p*m; absence has mass 1-p. The shared `DisplayMass` helper retains the general absent-empty-pool expression (1-p)+p*m, and finite tests cover forced outcomes too. The proposal's existing latent-branch math is retained for uncertified paths.

The source-reviewed first-three weak families have no escape or gold theft and use the standard gold range without custom proportions. The exact weak certificate fixes proportion1, so native A10 gold is uniform over the exact double-index preimages of 7..15. Runtime validation pins the hypothetical room, native encounter IdEntry, weak flag, absence of custom gold bounds/calculator, and actual proportion1. Unknown contexts continue to use the original all-float proportional-range envelope.

Native selection thresholds, candidate pools, potion forcing/threshold, and gold boundaries are checked against the detached certificate before conditioning. A hypothetical world with a different earlier history cannot borrow the certified factors. The original failure capture, alias protection, raw cursor accounting and completion guards remain in place. A partial rejected replay can have an incomplete likelihood product larger than a full-root envelope; such products are not evidence about correction acceptance.

## Shop boundary reuse

`TryRarityOffsetBeforeOwner` exposes the reconstructed float offset only immediately before a complete parentless Act0 Shop owner, after closing every prior target and all intervening observations. Merchant generation reads rarity pity without advancing it, so this is the proper seam for a fixed shop normalizer. Reward and OutsideChoice owners deliberately decline: those public owners can start after generation has already changed pity. A complete no-purchase ordinary shop can now preserve both reconstructed pity states for later reward targets. `NativePublicShopLeaveBoundary` is shared with the first-shop condition and deliberately constructs no probability or reward-history condition, avoiding recursion through `TryRarityOffsetBeforeOwner`.

This bridge requires a direct, complete Act0 map-to-Shop boundary, all fifteen ordered ordinary stock/action slots, exactly one initial stock snapshot, a single `leave` choice, a completed end with assets, and byte-equal detached assets before and after the shop. The existing inventory closure checks the actual card/relic/potion model types for room-entry and creation hooks, and the merchant closure checks `ModifyMerchantCardCreationResults` and `ModifyMerchantPrice`. The surrounding reward history still requires its narrower ordinary-relic and reward-hook closure. Purchases, removal, repeated/refreshed stock, restock paths, incomplete/missing boundaries, changed assets, and unreviewed modifiers retain universal suffix envelopes. This does not broaden the first-shop bag proposal's existing PreciseScissors/first-bag eligibility.

The pinned `MerchantInventory.GenerateCardEntries` calls `CardRarityOdds.RollWithoutChangingFutureOdds` for its five character slots; the two colorless slots do not roll rarity. Its separate upgrade rolls do not update rarity pity. `GeneratePotionEntries` calls the predicate overload of `PotionFactory.CreateRandomOutOfCombat`, which samples potion rarity and identity directly without calling `PotionRewardOdds.Roll`. Stock creation therefore consumes native random words and relic-bag positions while preserving both reward pity states. No stock factor is multiplied into the reward-history envelope; the shop proposal retains ownership of any stock conditioning.
